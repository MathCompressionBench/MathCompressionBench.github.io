Submit

  1. Get MathCompressionBench and the toy task.
  2. Compress proofs. ./check prints your node count.
  3. Run ./submit. Fork the submissions repository and keep your fork in sync with it, add your bundle with mcb intake add, and open a pull request from your fork: the steps.

Evaluation

Validity

  1. Target statements are unchanged.
  2. The target's definitions keep their meaning: the same types and values (proofs inside them may change).
  3. Lean's kernel accepts every declaration, re-checked in a sandbox.

Exact rule →

Metric

The score is the proof-term node count of the final proof: the nodes of the term Lean checks, each repeated subterm counted every time it occurs; lower is better.
